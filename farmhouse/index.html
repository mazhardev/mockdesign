Farm Cottage

A peaceful retreat in La Roche-en-Ardenne, opened to one party at a time — nine bedrooms for up to fifteen, a fireplace to gather round, and a terrace looking out on the Ardennes.

The house

An authentic Ardennes house, warm and generously sized, with charming stone features and daylight right through it. The large living room and its open fireplace is where everyone ends up in the evening; the terrace, just outside, is where the long shared meals happen.

Nine comfortable bedrooms sleep up to fifteen — the natural choice for groups. Guests have the run of the whole house, the large terrace and the private fenced garden, with spacious free parking right in front of the property.

The house sits within a private estate, so the quiet is real, and marked walking trails begin close by. Fast, free Wi-Fi reaches every room — for staying connected, or simply planning the day.

Everything is ready when you arrive: luxury bed and bath linen, for hotel-level comfort. Drop your bags and start enjoying the stay. We remain your point of contact throughout — for what is on around the estate, the best places locally, or bike hire for the marked trails.
