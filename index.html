Welcome

Bois Saint-Jean is a private estate in the Belgian Ardennes — four countryside homes, two meeting rooms, reception halls and a barn, set in acres of woodland, meadow and quiet ponds.

People come here to think, to make things, and to be together properly. Everything is arranged by conversation rather than by calendar.
