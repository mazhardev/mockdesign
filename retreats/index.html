The Ceramic Studio

A place to slow down and make something by hand.

The ceramic studio offers welcoming, creative workshops for complete beginners and more experienced makers. Come alone, bring friends or family, add a workshop to a retreat, or create an original company and team-building experience.

Sessions can be arranged privately or as a group workshop and adapted to the people taking part. No previous experience is needed—only curiosity and a willingness to work with clay.
