Spaces & Venue Hire

A setting for every kind of gathering

Bois Saint-Jean offers a collection of indoor and outdoor spaces for retreats, workshops, business gatherings and private events. Each can be hired individually or combined with accommodation and other parts of the estate, creating an experience shaped entirely around your plans.

One estate

Across a private estate of lush nature, ponds and peaceful seasonal landscapes, the spaces work together naturally while each retains its own atmosphere.

01 · The largest space

The Grange

A spacious and versatile venue beneath an impressive timber frame. The Grange adapts beautifully to receptions, conferences, workshops, retreats and private events, with enough scale to feel impressive and enough character to remain warm.

Capacity
150
Area
200 m²

Historic fireplaces · free high-speed Wi-Fi · price on request

Across the estate

Spaces with their own character

02 · Indoor

Salle des Traqueurs

A characterful reception and dining space beneath traditional vaulted ceilings. Stone walls, a fireplace and a practical bar area with a sink make it equally welcoming for shared meals, meetings, workshops and group activities.

Capacity 90 · fireplace · bar with sink · tableware · free Wi-Fi · price on request

03 · Catering

Professional Industrial Kitchen

A spacious shared kitchen with professional equipment, generous worktops and everything needed for retreats, catered events and hands-on cooking classes.

Professional equipment · cooking classes · event catering

04 · Meetings & workshops

The Bureau – Offices & Meeting Rooms

A dedicated building for meetings, workshops and smaller conferences, with private parking, a fully equipped kitchen, a private bathroom and two exclusive lounge areas. Breakfast and catering can be arranged around the day.

Combined capacity 30 · free high-speed Wi-Fi · private parking · price on request

05 · Business

Sand & Wood Meeting Rooms

Two distinct rooms, each welcoming up to 15 people. The light-filled Sand room and timber-framed Wood room both include a connected display, stationery and comfortable seating; the Wood room also has its own lounge.

15 people per room · connected displays · stationery · free Wi-Fi · price on request

06 · Creative workshops

Ceramics Workshop

A welcoming studio where beginners and experienced makers can design their own pieces or decorate ready-to-paint ceramics, with personalised guidance throughout the session.

Small groups of 4–6 · price on request

07 · Outdoors

Outdoor Event Space

A beautiful natural setting for long lunches, evening dinners and events beneath the trees. The format can evolve with the occasion and will be developed further as the estate’s outdoor programme grows.

Lunches · dinners · events in nature

08 · Outdoors

Wood-Fired Pizza Area

An informal outdoor space centred around the pleasure of cooking and eating together. The wood-fired oven makes it especially suited to relaxed evenings with a small group.

Ideal for around 7–8 people
