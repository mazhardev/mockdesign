The house

A spacious house of real character, standing on its own in the heart of the Ardennes — the place to gather, unwind and be outdoors. A large living room with fireplace, a fully equipped kitchen and a dining area generous enough to seat everyone at once. There is a dedicated workspace too, for the days that need one.

Six bedrooms give comfort and privacy to everyone in the party, and the games room — table tennis and foosball — tends to delight the adults as much as the children. Free, high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the estate, for staying connected, working remotely or planning the day.

Outside is a private garden and terrace kept for your use alone, with a barbecue and a long table for meals in the open air. The house sits within a private estate, so the quiet holds, and walking paths begin close by — La Roche-en-Ardenne and the Baraque de Fraiture are both near, for hiking and days out the year round.

Everything is ready when you arrive: luxury bed and bath linen, for hotel-level comfort. Drop your bags and start enjoying the stay. We remain your point of contact throughout — for what is on around the estate, the best places locally, or bike hire for the marked trails.
