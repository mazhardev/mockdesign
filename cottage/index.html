The house

A very old traditional Ardennes house, full of character and fully renovated, standing in an exceptional natural setting. Generous living spaces and a decor that blends rural charm with modern comfort — warm, spacious and authentic, and made for getting a family or a group of friends together.

The fully fenced private garden gives complete peace and privacy, and opens at the back onto a small stream running through the property — a soothing thing to have within earshot. A terrace and outdoor tables make the most of sunny days.

Four comfortable bedrooms make the house ideal for up to eight guests, balancing togetherness with privacy: everyone has their own comfortable space, with high-quality bedding and ample storage. Guests have the run of the whole house, the large terrace and the fenced garden, with spacious free parking right in front of the property.

The estate is surrounded by nature and threaded with marked walking trails — ask us and we will suggest walks made to suit you. Sheep are very likely to be grazing right beside the house, along with the wildlife of the surrounding forest. Luxury bed and bath linen is ready on arrival, and we remain your point of contact throughout, for bike hire or simply the best places locally.
