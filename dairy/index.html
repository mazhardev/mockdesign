The house

A former stable, fully renovated, set in its own green corner of a private and secure estate — bright and comfortable, surrounded by meadows, woodland and farm animals, with the forest in view from the windows. Everything here invites you to slow down, breathe and reconnect with nature. It suits a family or a small group of up to seven.

Two large bedrooms with super king-size beds, and a small office with a single sofa bed — right for an extra child, or for working remotely in peace. Downstairs there is a fully equipped kitchen, a laundry area, a bathroom and two separate toilets, and a cosy living and dining room with an open fireplace.

Families can travel light: a baby cot, high chair, potty, toys, books and children's tableware are all waiting. Private parking sits directly in front of the house, and fast, free Wi-Fi reaches the whole property. Outside, the setting is exceptional — the house faces a cross-country ski trail, walking paths are close by, and the garden and terrace are yours alone.

On the estate you can arrange a private pottery workshop or a therapeutic massage on request. Ten minutes away, La Roche-en-Ardenne has restaurants, shops and local activities, and the wider area holds a wildlife park, a water park, caves, a safari park and adventure days out. Luxury bed and bath linen is ready on arrival, and we remain your point of contact throughout — for bike hire, or simply the best places locally.
