Who we are

Bois Saint-Jean, Rue du Bois 00, 6800 Province de Luxembourg, Belgium. For anything to do with your data, write to enquiries@boissaintjean.be.

What we collect

Only what you send us through the enquiry form or by email: your name, email address, the dates and party size you mention, and your message. We do not sell or share it, and there is no marketing list unless you ask to be added.

Why we hold it

To answer your enquiry and, if you come to stay, to arrange your visit. Enquiries that come to nothing are deleted after twelve months; booking records are kept for six years for accounting.
