Les Bureaux · two rooms, up to 15 guests each

Where great ideas take shape

Designed for focus, creativity and meaningful collaboration, Les Bureaux brings the comfort of a considered workplace into the quiet of the Bois Saint-Jean Estate.

Independent, flexible and fully equipped

Set in a completely independent building, the workspaces are suited to board meetings, training sessions, workshops, coaching, strategy days and corporate retreats. Authentic Ardennes character sits alongside the practical amenities businesses expect, creating a setting that feels refined, welcoming and effortless from arrival to departure.

The Sand Meeting Room and the Wood Meeting Room each welcome up to fifteen guests. Book either room independently or reserve both for complete flexibility across different group sizes, breakout sessions and event formats.
